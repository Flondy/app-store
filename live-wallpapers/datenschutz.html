Dabei verarbeiten Google bzw. Apple technische Daten wie bei jedem Download aus ihrem Store. Wir erhalten diese Daten nicht.

2. Summary

The app requires no account or sign-in. It shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking services. We do not collect personal data through the app and do not transfer data to our own servers.

3. Data on your device

The app stores the following only locally on your device: favourites, your collections, recently viewed artworks, downloaded wallpapers, settings, your credit balance, unlocks and subscription status. This data is not sent to us and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If your operating system includes app data in a device backup (e.g. iCloud or Google backup), this happens outside the app under that service's settings and terms.

4. Purchases through Google Play or the App Store

Credit packs and the Premium subscription are processed by Google Play on Android and by Apple's App Store on iOS/iPadOS. You enter payment details only with Google or Apple; we never receive them. The app receives a purchase receipt (product, purchase time, transaction ID), verifies it on the device and adds the credits or unlocks Premium. The providers' privacy policies apply to their processing: Google · Apple.

5. Setting wallpapers, Photos and downloads

On Android the app uses the system wallpaper and live-wallpaper features. On iOS/iPadOS apps cannot set the wallpaper themselves, so at your request the app saves an artwork to the Photos app (add-only, no read access to your photos); iOS asks for your permission once. HD originals and live videos are downloaded on demand through Google Play (Play Asset Delivery) or Apple (On-Demand Resources); Google or Apple process technical data as with any store download. We do not receive this data.

6. External links

Links to social networks, the website or other apps open in the browser or store; the provider's privacy terms apply there.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

8. Your rights

As we do not collect personal data through the app, we hold no data about you. Contact: flondy.dev@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

If the app starts collecting data (e.g. a future account feature), we will update this policy beforehand.
